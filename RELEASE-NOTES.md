Daymark 1.1.5 adds drag-to-reorder checklist steps.

- Grab the six-dot handle beside a checklist step and drag it above or below another step. An insertion line shows its destination.
- Reorder steps while creating or editing a task. Saved order keeps each step's text and checked state together.
- Focus a handle and use the up/down arrow keys as a keyboard alternative.
- Repeating templates and individual occurrences keep their existing editing scopes.

In Daymark 1.1.3, open Settings & backups > Check for updates, download 1.1.5, and choose Install & restart. Your tasks and workspaces are preserved.

If still using Windows 1.1.0 or 1.1.1, close the app and run the new Windows installer over your existing installation folder once to receive the updater repair. Do not uninstall first.
